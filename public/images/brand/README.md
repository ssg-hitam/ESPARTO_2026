# Official Brand Assets Location

This directory is designated for the official ESPARTO 2026 and HITAM institutional brand assets.

## Required Official Assets:
1. `esparto-logo.svg` or `esparto-logo.png`: The official transparent ESPARTO 2026 emblem (featuring the gradient silhouette with illuminated brain filament).
2. `hitam-logo.svg` or `hitam-logo.png`: The official transparent crest/logo for Hyderabad Institute of Technology and Management (HITAM).
3. `favicon.ico` / `favicon.svg`: Official favicon suite for browser tabs and mobile bookmarks.

> **CRITICAL RULE**: Do NOT generate, redraw with CSS, or use AI-fabricated logos. Place only officially supplied, approved vector/transparent PNG assets here.
